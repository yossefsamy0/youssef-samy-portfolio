# Google Ads Search Campaign Plan — Local Service Business

**Type:** Practice campaign plan; not a live campaign  
**Scenario:** A local home-maintenance service wants qualified phone calls within a defined service area.

## Campaign objective
Generate relevant phone inquiries while filtering out job seekers, DIY research, and out-of-area traffic.

## Proposed structure
- Search | Emergency service | Exact and phrase match themes.
- Search | Core service | Exact and phrase match themes.
- Separate ad groups by service intent only when landing pages and budgets justify the split.

## Keyword research examples
Use location and service modifiers only after confirming the actual service area:
- [emergency home repair]
- "home maintenance service"
- [licensed technician near me]
- "book home repair"

These are seed terms, not verified search-volume or CPC findings.

## Negative keyword themes
jobs, careers, salary, course, training, DIY, free, used parts, wholesale — review search terms before applying; do not exclude a term if it could represent a valuable service request.

## Ad copy direction
**Headline ideas:** Book a Local Technician | Request a Service Visit | Home Maintenance Support  
**Description idea:** Tell us what needs fixing and your area. Call to check availability and service coverage.

Do not publish claims such as 24/7, licensed, guaranteed, or same-day unless the business can substantiate them.

## Location and schedule
Target only confirmed service locations. Use presence-based location settings where available and set ad schedule to staffed call-answering hours.

## Call conversion measurement
- Configure a call asset and/or call-focused ad format according to current account eligibility.
- Set a meaningful call duration threshold with the client.
- Track website click-to-call events where applicable.
- Test conversion actions before launch.
- Avoid counting every short or accidental call as a qualified lead.

## Optimization routine
Review search terms, location performance, device performance, call quality, missed calls, and conversion tracking at least weekly. Change one major variable at a time where practical.

## Launch checklist
1. Confirm service, coverage, hours, budget, and landing page.
2. Confirm phone number ownership and call-answering process.
3. Validate conversion actions.
4. Review policy compliance and ad claims.
5. Launch only after client approval.

No spend, leads, conversions, or ROI are claimed. A real campaign requires access to the client's Google Ads account and confirmed business details.
