# Social Media Marketing Case Study — CANADA Menswear

**Type:** Self-directed portfolio case study / sample strategy  
**Business context:** A fictionalized planning exercise for an Egyptian men's clothing brand preparing to sell online before relying on a physical storefront.

## Objective
Build an actionable first-month social media plan focused on product discovery, qualified messages, size/price questions, and order confirmation.

## Audience assumptions
- Men shopping for everyday and smart-casual clothing.
- Gift buyers who need clear product and sizing information.
- Local and nearby customers who can be served through delivery.

These are planning assumptions, not measured audience insights.

## Content pillars
1. Product details: fabric, fit, available sizes, colors, and price.
2. Styling: outfit combinations and occasions.
3. Trust: packing, delivery process, exchange policy, and real product photos.
4. Engagement: polls, questions, and customer FAQs.

## First-month cadence
- 3 feed posts per week.
- 2 short-form videos per week.
- Stories on active selling days.
- Reply to product inquiries during agreed business hours.

## Conversion workflow
Post or story → Direct message → Confirm product, size, color, and delivery area → Confirm total price and delivery fee → Record order → Follow up after delivery.

## Measurement
Track weekly: reach, profile visits, content interactions, inbound messages, qualified inquiries, confirmed orders, response time, and cost per qualified message when paid campaigns are running.

## Important
No campaign results are claimed in this case study. Replace assumptions with real product information, approved policies, and account insights before using it for a client.
